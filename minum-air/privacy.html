Privacy Policy

Minum Air · Meowie water reminder · Last updated 25 October 2026

The short version

  • Your profile and drink history stay on your phone.
  • Nothing is shared unless you turn on the friends leaderboard or lock-screen reminders.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • You can delete everything at any time from inside the app.

What stays on your phone

Your name, gender, age, weight, activity level, state, wake-up and sleep times, daily goal, drink log, chosen avatar and world, and app settings (language, sound, reminders). These are saved in the app's storage on your device only and are not sent to us.

Weather

To adjust your goal for the heat, the app asks Open-Meteo for the weather at the centre of the state you picked (for example "Penang"). It does not use your phone's GPS. Open-Meteo receives that general location and your internet address, as any website would.

Friends leaderboard (optional)

Only if you tap "Join the leaderboard", the app creates a 6-letter friend code and stores, on our server: your name, avatar number, today's water amount and goal, this week's and last week's daily progress, your streak, the number of days you reached your goal, and the codes of the friends you add. Your friends see these on the leaderboard. Anyone who knows your friend code can see them too, so only share it with people you trust. A secret key kept on your phone (stored on our side only as a one-way hash) is needed to change your record.

Lock-screen reminders (optional)

Only if you turn on reminders and allow notifications, the app stores on our server: a push address created by your browser, your name, language, wake-up and sleep times, reminder style, today's water amount and goal, the time of your last drink and when you last opened the app. This is used only to decide when to send you a reminder. Reminders are delivered through Google's (or your browser's) push service.

Where the data is kept

Leaderboard and reminder records are kept on Vercel (our hosting provider). Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

  • Everything: Profile → Delete all data. This erases the data on your phone, removes you from the leaderboard and stops lock-screen reminders.
  • Leaderboard only: Friends → tap your own row → Leave the leaderboard.
  • Reminders only: Profile → turn off Meowie reminders.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone. Leaderboard records that are no longer used may be removed by us.

If you can no longer open the app and want your leaderboard record deleted, contact us at the email address on our Google Play listing with your friend code and we will remove it.

Children

Minum Air is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Health note

Water goals are general guidance only and are not medical advice. If you have a health condition that affects how much you should drink, follow your doctor's advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
