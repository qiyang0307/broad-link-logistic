01 — Company

Broad-Link Logistics Sdn Bhd
Co. No. 636115-X
Johor Bahru, Johor

Broad-Link Logistics is a total logistics service provider headquartered in Johor Bahru. Since 2003 we have moved general cargo, bulk and chemical freight between Singapore and Malaysia — with transport, customs forwarding, warehousing and our own workshop under one roof, and service and customer satisfaction at the centre of everything we do.

Base

Strategically located in Johor Bahru — off Jalan Tampoi and in Sri Plentong — minutes from the Causeway and Second Link.

Fleet & workshop

Prime movers and trailers for general, bulk and chemical cargo, maintained by our in-house workshop.

Customs

Custom forwarding and cross-border documentation handled in-house for import & export.
